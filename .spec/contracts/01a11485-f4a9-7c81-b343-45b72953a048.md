## Notes

- Why a Contract: authentication and authorization cut across every server surface and Feature, and the host-local store and credential formats have their own version (`version: 1`) independent of the Workspace schema.
- There is no HTTP Contract (owner ruling Q4); the HTTP routes named here are the ones whose access rules are normative.
