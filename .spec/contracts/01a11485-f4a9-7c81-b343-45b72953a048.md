## Notes

- Why a Contract: authentication and authorization cut across every server surface and Feature, and the host-local store and credential formats have their own version (`version: 1`) independent of the Workspace schema.
- There is no HTTP Contract (owner ruling Q4); the HTTP routes named here are the ones whose access rules are normative.
- `<wsid>` in the store path is the hex SHA-256 of the Workspace root's real path; `<hint>` in credentials and the cookie name is the roster's 4-character hint. They are different values.
- A principal is what a request authenticates as: a member, or a system credential (01a11485-ebdd-70fc-b853-0621caf056b8).
- Discussion #139 expresses Product Kit access as the keys of 01a11bb1-b35a-7e69-bba5-978e3f47c4fa. Part 15 (Discussion #140) restates the role Clauses 01a11485-fa00-72da-bc46-98302a3c106e and 01a11485-fa44-7b6a-99f8-de4e1e8edcfc as keys and decides default grants; until then 01a11bb1-b427-777e-8175-fa24d61d434b maps the keys onto roles, and those role Clauses are not restated here.
