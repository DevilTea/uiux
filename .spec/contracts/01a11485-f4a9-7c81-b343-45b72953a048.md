## Notes

- 01a11c74-9c83-7d4e-83e4-840d420837a9 is satisfied by key-based authorization landing first ([code@98672a4 `src/application/access/policy.ts#L141`](https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/blob/98672a48d3d0babd56b8d471322cb3eea13f0d7f/src/application/access/policy.ts#L141) `authorizeOperation`), per [#140 owner rulings 2026-10-10: ruling 2](https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/discussions/140#discussioncomment-18851628): no operation that needs a Product Kit key existed before permission keys, so the transitional role layer was never built. Its retirement will be proposed after roster v2 lands (issue #142, https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/issues/142).
- Why a Contract: authentication and authorization cut across every server surface and Feature, and the host-local store and credential formats have their own version (`version: 2`) independent of the Workspace schema.
- There is no HTTP Contract (owner ruling Q4); the HTTP routes named here are the ones whose access rules are normative.
- `<wsid>` in the store path is the hex SHA-256 of the Workspace root's real path; `<hint>` in credentials and the cookie name is the roster's 4-character hint. They are different values.
- A principal is what a request authenticates as: a member, or a system credential (01a11485-ebdd-70fc-b853-0621caf056b8).
- Discussion #140 (Part 15) restates the role matrix as permission keys: 01a11485-fa00-72da-bc46-98302a3c106e, 01a11485-fa21-7b77-8ae5-1d7d0618e8a3, 01a11485-fa44-7b6a-99f8-de4e1e8edcfc, 01a11bb1-b35a-7e69-bba5-978e3f47c4fa and 01a11485-fa66-7cde-a10f-b8b796d01469 map keys to operations, while 01a11c09-a26e-73bb-9a29-eed40aae37bd and 01a11c09-a2db-74bb-abb3-1b8e24682221 own the key names, `humanOnly` flags and requirements. Discussion #139's interim role mapping, 01a11bb1-b427-777e-8175-fa24d61d434b, is replaced by the roster upgrade's role mapping, which by Discussion #140 decision 4 is also the initial grant of the Product Kit keys.
- 01a11485-fa00-72da-bc46-98302a3c106e places the version Resources (01a11a5e-27c1-77d1-b60d-45a5ccfc1ee7) under `history.read`. Discussion #140's key table lists only `list_versions` and `get_version_diff` there and "Resources" under `workspace.read`; the placement follows its rationale that history reads are apart from current reads.
- Owner ruling 2026-10-09 (https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/discussions/140#discussioncomment-18820361) settles the segment grammar of 01a11c09-a200-754a-ac85-48b52dfe7bdc, which Discussion #140 (decision 1, A; R2) left open: every segment starts with a lowercase letter, digits may follow (`oauth2.read`), and `-` stays inside a segment, so `views.2` and `2fa.read` are not key names.
