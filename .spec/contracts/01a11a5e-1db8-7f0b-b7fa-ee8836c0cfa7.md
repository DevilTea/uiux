## Notes

- Why a Contract: these records are persisted inside the Workspace and on the host, are shared by the timeline, checkpoint, comparison and restore Features, are read by Agents and by other UIUX builds, and evolve under their own `historySchemaVersion`, independently of any one Feature.
- Decision 6 calls its physical layout, and #122 calls its Schema delta, non-normative sketches; the table "What `.spec/` records after acceptance" assigns the record shapes, both store paths, `historySchemaVersion`, the name and note limits, the grouping and retention values and the actor types to this Contract, so they are recorded here as the sketch names them. Left to implementation: the file modes of the host store, the format of `open.json` beyond holding the open autosave's events, a `displayName` for system actors, whether host versions also carry `source`, and the field shapes of a semantic diff.
