title: Workspace persisted format
summary: "The persisted format of a UIUX Workspace, versioned as a whole by the
  manifest `schemaVersion`: the Workspace layout and its data directories, the
  manifest and the Product Kit file, the path, file unit and identity of every
  canonical resource kind and of the content-addressed derived-artifact store,
  the canonical JSON shapes of those resources, the migration steps, and the
  diagnostics of a Workspace whose schema is older or unsupported."

      subdirectory. In the old layout, below `schemaVersion` 5, the manifest is
      `.uiux/workspace.json` under the Workspace root, and `.uiux/` holds UIUX
      system metadata.
