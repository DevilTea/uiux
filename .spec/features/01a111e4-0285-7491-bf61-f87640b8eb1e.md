---
id: 01a111e4-0285-7491-bf61-f87640b8eb1e
title: Member roster and roles
summary: "The host-local member roster of each Workspace: where it is kept and
  how it is protected, members with their kinds and cumulative roles,
  bootstrapping a first Owner, carrying a roster over to a moved Workspace, and
  managing it from the CLI and the Workbench."
rules:
  - id: 01a11485-e9f5-79d8-a517-9d7e01321188
    statement: Each Workspace has exactly one roster on the host, identified by the
      real path of its root, so a symlink and its target share it; no roster
      serves two Workspaces, so the same person in two Workspaces is two
      independent members, possibly with different roles.
  - id: 01a11485-ea1b-7014-9111-62ca6c243342
    statement: A roster lives only on the host, under `$UIUX_HOME`, never in the
      Workspace or in Git, and it changes no canonical file, manifest field or
      `schemaVersion`; a copy of a Workspace therefore carries no members or
      credentials.
  - id: 01a11485-ea39-7069-abc5-410b7576f445
    statement: UIUX refuses a roster store that is a symlink, that is writable by
      group or others, or that records a Workspace root other than the one being
      served, and never silently reuses such a store.
  - id: 01a114ec-df62-74ec-bf6d-2571137c3576
    statement: Every roster write is atomic under the store's lock.
  - id: 01a11485-ea58-782e-9789-138833c868d9
    statement: "The CLI and a running server may both write a roster: the server
      re-reads it when it changes, at the check interval the Access Contract
      sets, so a CLI revoke, removal or role change takes effect on its next
      request without a restart."
  - id: 01a11485-ea75-7964-bc21-29aa78049abf
    statement: A member's kind, human or Agent, is fixed at creation, so an Agent
      can never be turned into a human to gain human-only powers.
  - id: 01a114ec-dfd0-7755-8454-64174ff0f327
    statement: A member's nickname is unique in its roster, ignoring case, and is
      its display name everywhere.
  - id: 01a11485-ea91-7107-b02e-f3db764e6d86
    statement: An Owner may rename a member or change its role; records written
      earlier keep the name they were written with.
  - id: 01a11485-eaac-7523-998b-26123d7618df
    statement: "Roles are cumulative, Viewer ⊂ Reviewer ⊂ Editor ⊂ Owner: each role
      may do everything the roles below it may do (the Access Contract lists
      which operations need which role)."
  - id: 01a11485-eac6-730b-b66b-309cc9efb169
    statement: "Agents are capped at Editor, so minting credentials and changing
      roles stay with humans: an Agent can never be made Owner."
  - id: 01a11485-eae3-7080-8f02-da27fc260e09
    statement: A roster's last human Owner cannot be removed or demoted.
  - id: 01a11485-eaff-7339-91f9-10e9ea614b5b
    statement: Removing a member also removes its Tokens, invites and sessions, so
      none of them works after the server's next roster reload.
  - id: 01a11485-eb1a-77fb-a91e-b54c505344da
    statement: When `uiux dev` opens a roster that has no human Owner, it creates
      one named after the sanitized OS user name (falling back to `owner`) and
      prints an invite sign-in link for it with the loopback URL and the
      commands for adding an Agent.
  - id: 01a114ec-e085-7353-bb10-6fa44d0e8cd5
    statement: Later starts print no bootstrap link, and startup never prints a Token.
  - id: 01a11485-eb35-70b8-8c6b-b975f20f5b29
    statement: "UIUX does not detect a moved or renamed Workspace or a git worktree:
      a Workspace at a new real path starts with an empty roster and bootstraps
      its own Owner, unless an old roster is carried over with `uiux access
      copy`."
  - id: 01a11485-eb50-7f42-85c4-6e524cb9c4b4
    statement: "A copied roster is independent of its source: it records the new
      Workspace root and gets its own roster hint, it carries no invites,
      sessions or last-used times, and later changes in either roster never
      reach the other."
  - id: 01a11485-eb6b-724e-b1cc-3f2bc59367e4
    statement: The roster's recorded Workspace root and hint are shown in the `uiux
      dev` startup output, by `uiux member list` and on the Workbench Members
      page.
  - id: 01a11485-eb86-7a69-bf1b-70d492ee3be4
    statement: "On the loopback listener, a signed-in human Owner manages the served
      Workspace's roster on the Workbench Members page: adding, renaming,
      changing the role of and removing members, creating Tokens (with a copy
      action) and revoking them, creating invites, and listing and revoking
      sessions."
  - id: 01a11485-eba3-7c07-a94f-0d69e610a0b6
    statement: The Workbench hides the actions the signed-in member's role cannot
      perform, or disables them and names the required role; the server stays
      the authority.
