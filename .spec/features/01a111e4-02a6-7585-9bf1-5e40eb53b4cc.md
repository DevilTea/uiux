title: Baseline request gates
summary: "Baseline gates (Host, Origin, `Sec-Fetch-Site`, Content-Type and
  framing) run first on both listeners, before authentication: the `Host` check
  applies to every request, the `Origin` and `Sec-Fetch-Site` checks only to
  state-changing methods (anything but GET, HEAD and OPTIONS) and `/mcp` paths,
  and the Content-Type check only to state-changing methods; the loopback
  listener is the default bind, and the LAN listener adds its own `Host`
  allowlist. A foreign `Host` gets 421 `request.host_rejected`, a cross-origin
  or cross-site state-changing or `/mcp` request 403 `request.origin_rejected`
  or `request.cross_site_rejected`, a state-changing request with a non-JSON
  body 415 `request.content_type_rejected`, and responses forbid framing by
  other origins with `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'self'` and
  `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`."
