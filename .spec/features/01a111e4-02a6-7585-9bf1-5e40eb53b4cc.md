title: Baseline request gates
summary: "Baseline gates (Host, Origin, `Sec-Fetch-Site`, Content-Type and
  framing) run first on both listeners, before authentication: they refuse a
  foreign `Host`, browser cross-origin state-changing or `/mcp` requests,
  non-JSON mutation bodies and framing by other origins; the loopback listener
  is the default bind, and the LAN listener adds its own `Host` allowlist. A
  foreign `Host` gets 421 `request.host_rejected`, a cross-origin or cross-site
  request 403 `request.origin_rejected` or `request.cross_site_rejected`, a
  non-JSON mutation body 415 `request.content_type_rejected`, and responses
  forbid framing by other origins with `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors
  'self'` and `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`."
