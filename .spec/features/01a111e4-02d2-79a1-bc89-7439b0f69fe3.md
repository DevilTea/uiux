title: Server-stamped actors
summary: Review actors and times written through `/api/*` and `/mcp` are stamped
  by the server from the authenticated member as `member:<uuid>`; a
  caller-supplied `actor` or `at` is ignored with a warning, and history is not
  rewritten.
rules:
  - id: 01a11485-f134-71cb-ba9a-9b5709e092dc
    statement: On `/api/*` and `/mcp`, every timeline-writing Review mutation
      (reply, re-anchor, mark ready, resolve, reopen and message edit) and the
      Decision provenance written by promotion take their actor from the
      authenticated member, never from the request.
  - id: 01a11485-f14e-74bd-be57-c20e26ca047b
    statement: "A request-supplied `actor` is optional and ignored: when it differs
      from the stamp, the write still succeeds and the result carries an
      `auth.actor_ignored` warning."
  - id: 01a11485-f16a-7ca9-be79-a573298d4d23
    statement: "The authored time of those records is the server's clock: a supplied
      `at` is ignored with an `auth.time_ignored` warning, while in-process
      callers of the domain services, such as tests and migration, still pass
      their own."
  - id: 01a11485-f185-7c23-8f2a-e045a279cd27
    statement: "Stamping never rewrites history: records written before keep their
      caller-asserted actors, and a removed member's records keep its member ID
      and recorded display name, which the Workbench marks as removed."
  - id: 01a11485-f1a0-7a4c-90d5-971d6d7ddfc6
    statement: A stamp shows that the write came through a credential issued to that
      member, not who held the credential.
