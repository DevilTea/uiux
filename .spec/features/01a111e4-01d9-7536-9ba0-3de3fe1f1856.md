title: Review message editing
summary: "Editing Review messages: who may edit and until when, how earlier
  versions are kept, and how edits appear in the Workbench."
rules:
  - id: 01a11544-5798-7401-bdaa-4dce41a6fc86
    statement: Only a message's author, human or Agent, may replace its text; no
      other member, an Owner included, may edit it.
  - id: 01a11544-57bd-72a8-bbdc-24819db114ba
    statement: Authorship is proven by the edit's stamped actor matching the
      message's actor, so a message written before stamping or by a removed
      member can never be edited.
  - id: 01a11544-57e2-701c-946f-3fe75f5294ba
    statement: A message is frozen permanently once a ready-for-review submission or
      a resolution exists at or after its authored time; reopening the thread
      never unfreezes it.
  - id: 01a11544-5808-772e-800c-ed37332596e1
    statement: Messages written after the latest submission or resolution stay
      editable, even on a resolved thread.
  - id: 01a11544-5830-7e3a-91cb-c0e24320befb
    statement: Apart from the freeze, editing a message has no time limit.
  - id: 01a11544-5855-75ad-ba0c-4132b19973ec
    statement: Each edit keeps the replaced text append-only on the message, with
      the edit's server-stamped actor and time.
  - id: 01a11544-587e-74a3-96b8-cf85473bed0f
    statement: An edited message keeps its authored time and timeline position; an
      edit adds no history event and no separate timeline item.
  - id: 01a11544-58a4-7d7e-b7c1-4491b736d146
    statement: No Review action deletes a single message.
  - id: 01a11544-58ca-7fda-9bd6-bf2e9853ef90
    statement: An edited thread shows as unread to members who saw it before the edit.
  - id: 01a11544-58f0-76eb-82e3-2281f399bb20
    statement: Search matches a message's current text only, never its earlier versions.
  - id: 01a11544-5916-774b-a7e3-529bd29eb1ea
    statement: The Workbench marks an edited message as edited, with the edit time,
      and lets every reader of the thread open its earlier versions, newest
      first, read-only.
  - id: 01a11544-593c-787b-9d25-34f9745ee674
    statement: On the viewer's own frozen message, the Workbench shows Edit as
      unavailable with the reason.
  - id: 01a11544-5964-7c23-92e6-0ab5e502a5af
    statement: Editing a message never changes a Decision promoted from its thread,
      and promotion does not freeze messages.
