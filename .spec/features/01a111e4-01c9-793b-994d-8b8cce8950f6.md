---
id: 01a111e4-01c9-793b-994d-8b8cce8950f6
title: Review lifecycle and resolution
summary: "The lifecycle of a Review thread: ready-for-review submissions and
  their Evidence gate, resolution and its kinds, reopening, and how the
  Workbench offers resolution."
rules:
  - id: 01a11544-5434-7604-b573-b3f3da49024a
    statement: Every lifecycle transition appends an immutable event to the thread's
      history, and the thread's current status always matches the latest one.
  - id: 01a11544-5459-73a9-89d2-dd4d3ed42da3
    statement: Marking a thread ready for review records a new immutable submission;
      it means work was submitted, not that it was accepted.
  - id: 01a11544-547e-7dc6-93f0-abffffd8fe78
    statement: Every entry into ready for review, including one after a reopen,
      appends a new submission and never replaces an earlier one.
  - id: 01a11544-54a8-79f0-a590-e8f8f42c1a79
    statement: Once a submission exists, its change domains, resources and Evidence
      references never change.
  - id: 01a11544-54ce-7a05-98ab-c9e66877b30b
    statement: A Widget thread can enter ready for review only while its anchored
      View is valid as a whole and every named Variant is executable.
  - id: 01a11544-54f5-76e0-b5ee-b137d387f08e
    statement: A Widget thread's submission names its anchored View at that View's
      current revision.
  - id: 01a11544-551c-7608-86bd-24eeca2fdda9
    statement: A Workspace thread's submission names the current Workspace manifest
      revision as its subject.
  - id: 01a11544-5541-77c2-9011-0c179613c3f8
    statement: Every View a Workspace thread's submission names must exist, be valid
      and be named at its current revision.
  - id: 01a11544-5564-7716-8552-e57ec5795644
    statement: Every Evidence reference of a submission must name an Evidence record
      that exists in the Workspace.
  - id: 01a11544-558b-7b86-b4c7-76928862c948
    statement: Formal-capture Evidence cited by a submission must be complete for
      the current revision of the View it captured, and that View must belong to
      the submission's target set.
  - id: 01a11544-55b0-7986-a32a-2a4fea7135d4
    statement: An Evidence reference whose declared kind differs from the referenced
      record's kind is invalid and diagnosed, never coerced or rewritten.
  - id: 01a11544-55d5-7ee4-9d63-327595144bdc
    statement: A visual or interaction change cites Playwright captures of the clean
      Preview Host in the affected render and Variant contexts; a purely textual
      Spec change needs no capture but still needs passing validation.
  - id: 01a11544-55fa-7b7e-aec5-e71b1ba9a214
    statement: An interaction change cites replayable interaction Evidence of
      structured steps, targets and assertions replayed with Playwright;
      screenshots alone never verify interaction.
  - id: 01a11544-5621-71c6-b5b9-ad1b1ef809ab
    statement: Resolving a ready-for-review thread without naming a resolution
      resolves it as verified, accepting its latest submission.
  - id: 01a11544-5647-747f-bea3-5cd9d92241b8
    statement: Resolving an open thread requires an explicit resolution; the API
      never chooses one for the caller.
  - id: 01a11544-566b-7472-8e4d-b20f5e348222
    statement: Closing a ready-for-review thread with any resolution other than
      verified leaves its pending submission in the thread, not accepted.
  - id: 01a11544-5690-735b-b8f5-14aa9994a52b
    statement: "UIUX never checks a resolution against other resources: whether a
      thread is obsolete or a duplicate is the resolving human's judgment."
  - id: 01a11544-56b5-78ff-bce6-ed5183a13ad2
    statement: Reopening a thread, whatever its resolution, keeps its earlier
      lifecycle events and submissions unchanged in its history.
  - id: 01a11544-56db-73f1-addf-96c97320e8ee
    statement: "After a reopen, a verified resolution needs a new submission: an
      earlier verified resolution is never carried forward."
  - id: 01a11544-5702-78b6-95ea-c15fe80ad4e3
    statement: In the Workbench, the one-click Resolve resolves an open thread as
      answered and accepts a ready-for-review thread's submission as verified,
      naming that submission.
  - id: 01a11544-5727-7d41-b732-3108b15a8ec1
    statement: The Workbench's Resolve menu groups answered and verified under
      Resolve, and obsolete, duplicate and won't-fix under Dismiss; the grouping
      is presentation only.
  - id: 01a11544-574d-7a9f-9eae-f63f96365944
    statement: For a thread whose anchor is invalid, the Workbench offers resolving
      it as obsolete but never chooses that resolution without the user.
  - id: 01a11544-5772-750b-9ab3-adcc04d755a9
    statement: The timeline shows each resolution with its kind, and marks a
      submission declined by a non-verified close as not accepted.
