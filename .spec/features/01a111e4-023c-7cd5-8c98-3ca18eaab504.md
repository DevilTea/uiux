title: Widget navigation and highlight
summary: Navigating to a Widget selects it in the structure and, when it is
  visible, scrolls to and highlights it; the Workbench draws the highlight from
  versioned, unscaled iframe geometry, shows only the parts that survive
  clipping and occlusion, and falls back predictably when the target View or
  Widget no longer exists.
