---
id: 01a111e4-0328-7e20-aebf-15b78c5fa37f
title: Workbench information architecture
summary: "The Workbench's places and navigation: primary and secondary
  navigation, Overview and its tabs, the single View page, the Workspace pages,
  the Reviews inbox with its queue, tabs, filters and thread links, the command
  palette, the Members entry, deep links, how threads recorded in another render
  context appear and open, and the persistence of the Comment tool."
rules:
  - id: 01a116f0-8927-7854-b67d-e384cde6e6b1
    statement: The primary navigation is Overview, UX Flows and Reviews, the same
      three destinations in the desktop sidebar, the tablet icon rail and the
      phone bottom bar.
  - id: 01a116f0-8973-70c5-980e-4c851d61f42c
    statement: Overview's Views tab is the only page that lists every View; the
      sidebar's View list on a View page only switches between Views.
  - id: 01a116f0-89b6-7d08-a850-e1ee1aecd51f
    statement: "A View's page is its review workspace: it switches Views and
      Variants, chooses the Locale, viewport and theme of the render context,
      and shows the View's Spec and Decisions, its Review threads and the UX
      Flows that use it."
  - id: 01a116f0-8a01-7cd2-807d-cc26f7bf8b16
    statement: Workspace is one secondary navigation entry below the primary ones,
      with its own sub-navigation of Settings, Product Kit, Locales and Assets.
  - id: 01a116f0-8a46-76bc-a29f-affaa302c44c
    statement: Adapters are managed in the Overview section of the Product Kit page,
      neither in Workspace Settings nor on a page of their own.
  - id: 01a116f0-8a8b-787d-b5d4-34e1325bc845
    statement: The retired addresses of the View list and of Adapters keep working
      by opening their replacements, Overview's Views tab and the Overview
      section of the Product Kit page.
  - id: 01a116f0-8acf-7788-92cc-8d207694d343
    statement: Prototype is a mode launched from a UX Flow, never a navigation
      destination of its own.
  - id: 01a116f0-8b11-7eba-96eb-786ba7098589
    statement: The Component Catalog is a secondary area, never part of the primary
      navigation.
  - id: 01a116f0-8b54-71f1-8dd4-39699b8cc5f9
    statement: Checks findings and recent activity are tabs of Overview beside its
      Views tab, with no navigation destination of their own.
  - id: 01a116f0-8bda-7a63-99b2-fa3b98b2b336
    statement: Switching Overview's tab updates its address without adding a browser
      history entry.
  - id: 01a116f0-8c1d-7bed-803f-319d8cc5c10d
    statement: Following a link to a Workspace Settings section scrolls to that
      section, also when the address already names it.
  - id: 01a116f0-8c67-70e5-885b-d7b7f28feafb
    statement: A command palette, open from every Workbench page, jumps to each
      navigation destination and Workspace page and finds any View, UX Flow or
      Review thread by search.
  - id: 01a116f0-8caf-7e03-ba40-ef17e116aa22
    statement: Owners reach the Members page from the member menu, and members of
      other roles see no Members entry.
  - id: 01a116f0-8cf2-7591-be25-3f6e600175fc
    statement: Reviews is the global inbox of every Review thread in the Workspace,
      Widget threads and Workspace threads alike.
  - id: 01a116f0-8d33-7c2a-ba50-fc177e9deda1
    statement: The inbox's default queue hides resolved threads and lists threads
      that are ready for review before open ones.
  - id: 01a116f0-8d77-7665-8e2a-5560b6ab660f
    statement: Within each status group, the inbox orders threads by latest
      activity, newest first.
  - id: 01a116f0-8db9-75c3-a642-652570053138
    statement: Resolved and dismissed threads stay reachable from the inbox through
      an explicit tab or filter.
  - id: 01a116f0-8dfb-7430-b056-c9cf497d022f
    statement: The inbox lists the threads resolved with a Dismiss group resolution
      in their own Dismissed tab, and its Resolved tab lists only those resolved
      with a Resolve group resolution.
  - id: 01a116f0-8e3e-7b1f-a12a-79fdcaccbf62
    statement: The inbox narrows its queue by the structured Review filters, and
      also by thread author, by threads the member took part in, by threads
      changed since the member last saw them and by text.
  - id: 01a116f0-8e81-76ba-850c-0cbc42fbc351
    statement: The inbox's filter and open thread are part of its address, so a
      copied inbox link reopens the same queue and thread.
  - id: 01a116f0-8ec3-7eff-9e61-4e1b3b31dabe
    statement: From the inbox, a Widget thread opens on its View in the Variant and
      render context it concerns, with the thread open and its Widget selected
      while the anchor is valid.
  - id: 01a118a1-9e11-7933-9123-4ba03f0ac223
    statement: Opening a thread from the inbox never changes the chrome language or
      theme, even when the thread records a different Locale or theme.
  - id: 01a1170f-c0ce-70ba-a94b-ff3cf24c38b0
    statement: A thread started on the canvas, a comment on the whole View included,
      records the Preview's current Locale, viewport and theme.
  - id: 01a1170f-c11d-7d39-a5ce-cfe9ffdff6ed
    statement: A thread's header shows the render context it records and offers to
      open the thread in the current context instead.
  - id: 01a1170f-c165-7dd4-bc9f-a799f7884f14
    statement: When a recorded Locale, viewport or theme no longer exists, the
      Workbench opens the thread with the default for it and the other recorded
      members, and shows a non-blocking notice naming it.
  - id: 01a1170f-c1ae-78fc-98cb-dc8f4b97f98f
    statement: On the canvas, a thread whose recorded render context differs from
      the current one keeps its otherwise visible pin and bubble, muted and
      labeled with that context.
  - id: 01a1170f-c1f7-7e54-8754-c1a047415d4e
    statement: Activating a muted pin or bubble switches the Preview render context
      to the thread's recorded members, keeping the chrome, the Variant and
      every member the thread does not record, and opens the thread.
  - id: 01a1170f-c23d-7318-b035-f84526e4b98f
    statement: Only the members a thread records are compared with the current
      render context, so a member it does not record never makes it differ, and
      a thread without a recorded render context is never muted.
  - id: 01a1170f-c282-7dcc-9b16-e8dfac21a348
    statement: Muting never shows a pin that another pin rule hides and never moves a pin.
  - id: 01a116f0-8f07-79c1-9e74-1ac265b5e04a
    statement: A Workspace thread has no canvas link; its link opens it in the
      Reviews inbox.
  - id: 01a116f0-8f4c-7fac-a46d-53d8dba61dfa
    statement: A link to a View carries its render context, selected Widget, open
      thread and panel, so opening it reproduces what its author saw.
  - id: 01a116f0-8f90-7a3e-a71b-cd5cb22482ce
    statement: After a comment target is committed, Comment mode stays on for the
      next comment until the user leaves it.
  - id: 01a11a5e-1946-7217-aff5-db13df3a2977
    statement: "Overview's Activity tab is the version timeline: versions newest
      first and grouped by day, with autosave, checkpoint, external and system
      rows, where an autosave row expands to its write events."
  - id: 01a11a5e-199c-712f-b982-bb506c4954c8
    statement: The Activity timeline filters by actor, by resource kind and to
      checkpoints only.
  - id: 01a11a5e-19f4-77a0-8e8b-30ea280b7b24
    statement: The Activity timeline collapses an autosave with no net change.
  - id: 01a11a5e-1a4b-72f7-8de9-1fc528aaf6eb
    statement: Activity's list of changes since this browser last looked links each
      change to a comparison from the version holding the last-seen revision to
      the current state.
  - id: 01a11a5e-1aa5-7d13-939a-8d99245f7906
    statement: A View's page offers a history side panel that lists the versions
      that changed the View.
  - id: 01a11a5e-1afd-79e8-bfe8-8c0eccfaa396
    statement: The selected version and comparison are part of the address, so a
      copied link reopens the same comparison.
  - id: 01a11bb1-b918-7cb5-b581-a3fd3f647f09
    statement: The Product Kit page has the sections Overview, with packages, the
      build and Adapters; Components, with the registry, the component table,
      component browsing with props and emits, the Catalog and copy commands;
      Styles & themes; and Files, read-only.
