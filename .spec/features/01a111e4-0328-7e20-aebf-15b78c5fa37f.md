summary: "The Workbench's places and navigation: primary and secondary
  navigation, Overview and its tabs, the single View page, the Workspace pages,
  the Reviews inbox with its queue, tabs, filters and thread links, the command
  palette, the Members entry, deep links and the persistence of the Comment
  tool."
rules:
  - id: 01a116f0-8927-7854-b67d-e384cde6e6b1
    statement: The primary navigation is Overview, UX Flows and Reviews, the same
      three destinations in the desktop sidebar, the tablet icon rail and the
      phone bottom bar.
  - id: 01a116f0-8973-70c5-980e-4c851d61f42c
    statement: Overview's Views tab is the Workbench's one list of every View.
  - id: 01a116f0-89b6-7d08-a850-e1ee1aecd51f
    statement: "A View's page is its review workspace: it switches Views and
      Variants, chooses the Locale, viewport and theme of the render context,
      and shows the View's Spec and Decisions, its Review threads and the UX
      Flows that use it."
  - id: 01a116f0-8a01-7cd2-807d-cc26f7bf8b16
    statement: Workspace is one secondary navigation entry below the primary ones,
      with its own sub-navigation of Settings, Locales and Assets.
  - id: 01a116f0-8a46-76bc-a29f-affaa302c44c
    statement: Adapters are a section of Workspace Settings, not a page of their own.
  - id: 01a116f0-8a8b-787d-b5d4-34e1325bc845
    statement: The retired addresses of the View list and of Adapters keep working
      by opening their replacements, Overview's Views tab and the Adapters
      section of Settings.
  - id: 01a116f0-8acf-7788-92cc-8d207694d343
    statement: Prototype is a mode launched from a UX Flow, never a navigation
      destination of its own.
  - id: 01a116f0-8b11-7eba-96eb-786ba7098589
    statement: The Component Catalog is a secondary area, never part of the primary
      navigation.
  - id: 01a116f0-8b54-71f1-8dd4-39699b8cc5f9
    statement: Checks findings and recent activity are tabs of Overview beside its
      Views tab, with no navigation destination of their own.
  - id: 01a116f0-8b96-7827-881d-117b4f906ecb
    statement: Overview shows its Views tab whenever its address names no tab, and
      returning to the Views tab removes the tab from the address.
  - id: 01a116f0-8bda-7a63-99b2-fa3b98b2b336
    statement: Switching Overview's tab updates its address without adding a browser
      history entry.
  - id: 01a116f0-8c1d-7bed-803f-319d8cc5c10d
    statement: Following a link to a Workspace Settings section scrolls to that
      section, also when the address already names it.
  - id: 01a116f0-8c67-70e5-885b-d7b7f28feafb
    statement: A command palette, open from every Workbench page, jumps to each
      navigation destination and Workspace page and finds Views, UX Flows and
      Review threads by name.
  - id: 01a116f0-8caf-7e03-ba40-ef17e116aa22
    statement: Owners reach the Members page from the member menu, and members of
      other roles see no Members entry.
  - id: 01a116f0-8cf2-7591-be25-3f6e600175fc
    statement: Reviews is the global inbox of every Review thread in the Workspace,
      Widget threads and Workspace threads alike.
  - id: 01a116f0-8d33-7c2a-ba50-fc177e9deda1
    statement: The inbox's default queue hides resolved threads and lists threads
      that are ready for review before open ones.
  - id: 01a116f0-8d77-7665-8e2a-5560b6ab660f
    statement: Within each status group, the inbox orders threads by latest
      activity, newest first.
  - id: 01a116f0-8db9-75c3-a642-652570053138
    statement: Resolved and dismissed threads stay reachable from the inbox through
      an explicit tab or filter.
  - id: 01a116f0-8dfb-7430-b056-c9cf497d022f
    statement: The inbox lists threads resolved as obsolete, duplicate or won't-fix
      as Dismissed in their own tab, and its Resolved tab lists only answered
      and verified threads.
  - id: 01a116f0-8e3e-7b1f-a12a-79fdcaccbf62
    statement: The inbox narrows its queue by the structured Review filters, and
      also by thread author, by threads the member took part in, by threads
      changed since the member last saw them and by text.
  - id: 01a116f0-8e81-76ba-850c-0cbc42fbc351
    statement: The inbox's filter and open thread are part of its address, so a
      copied inbox link reopens the same queue and thread.
  - id: 01a116f0-8ec3-7eff-9e61-4e1b3b31dabe
    statement: From the inbox, a Widget thread opens on its View in the Variant and
      render context it concerns, with the thread open and its Widget selected
      while the anchor is valid.
  - id: 01a116f0-8f07-79c1-9e74-1ac265b5e04a
    statement: A Workspace thread has no canvas link; its link opens it in the
      Reviews inbox.
  - id: 01a116f0-8f4c-7fac-a46d-53d8dba61dfa
    statement: A link to a View carries its render context, selected Widget, open
      thread and panel, so opening it reproduces what its author saw, with
      omitted values meaning the current defaults.
  - id: 01a116f0-8f90-7a3e-a71b-cd5cb22482ce
    statement: After a comment target is committed, Comment mode stays on for the
      next comment until the user leaves it.
