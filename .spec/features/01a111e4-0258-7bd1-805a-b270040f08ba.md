title: Visible Widget geometry
summary: "Widget geometry in the Workbench: per-Widget geometry streams and the
  tracked-Widget cap, the mapping from the Preview content box to the overlay,
  pin tracking and visibility, and visible-region contours with their precision,
  caching and numeric conformance."
rules:
  - id: 01a11658-2350-7cfb-8494-8f51efd3f991
    statement: The Workbench maps reported geometry into its overlay from the
      browser's actual rendering of the Preview content box, treating configured
      zoom or scale values as inputs, never as the mapping itself.
  - id: 01a11658-2396-777b-8cad-5c0d947171fb
    statement: Moving, scaling, zooming or transforming the Preview from outside,
      scrolling the Workbench or changing the iframe border only reprojects the
      latest accepted geometry; it never requests new geometry or advances a
      revision.
  - id: 01a11658-23dd-781d-a0d3-c45cba9446b0
    statement: When the Preview content viewport changes size, the Workbench hides
      every highlight and pin at once and reopens every geometry stream under a
      fresh identity, so geometry measured before the resize never shows.
  - id: 01a11658-2426-777f-8c5f-1b9185054975
    statement: When the Workbench cannot reliably measure how the Preview is
      rendered, it draws no highlight, outline or pin, substitutes no bounding
      box, configured value or last-known mapping, and keeps the structural
      selection.
  - id: 01a11658-246f-778d-b267-a8efa4541d60
    statement: Without a browser API that reports the content-box quad, the
      Workbench accepts its box measurement only after proving every transform
      up the flat tree is a translation with uniform positive scale; any failed
      proof, including an SVG ancestor, means unmeasurable.
  - id: 01a11658-24b8-7910-a7f7-b10b04496a23
    statement: "The Preview measures Widget and occluder boxes under the same proof:
      a Widget outside it reports no visible region and an occluder outside it
      counts as unsupported, so rotated or skewed Widgets get no highlight or
      pin."
  - id: 01a11658-2500-71b1-94b5-88e83fbbc674
    statement: The Workbench tracks outer-mapping changes by events and measures the
      rendered mapping at most once per animation frame per Preview, shared by
      every overlay, with no frame loop while no overlay is shown.
  - id: 01a11658-2545-768a-8d08-2b1547fe7d24
    statement: While pins are the only overlay, the Workbench measures only after a
      change signal or during an animation, so shown pins with nothing changing
      cost no frame callbacks and no messages.
  - id: 01a11658-2588-7f50-b8d5-ffcd4d117e41
    statement: "The Workbench keeps at most one geometry stream per Widget and
      shares its reports with every consumer of that Widget: the highlight, the
      hover outline, pins and a pending composer."
  - id: 01a11658-25cb-7c05-ab57-3d57883de94d
    statement: When demand exceeds the stream cap, streams go first to the open or
      selected thread, the pending composer and the hover candidate, then the
      selection highlight, then pinned Widgets by thread status, newest activity
      and Widget identity.
  - id: 01a11658-2611-7395-92f0-372dd841ed6a
    statement: A thread whose Widget gets no stream draws no pin, never a last-known
      one, and is listed as not shown on the canvas with a hint to narrow the
      pin filter.
  - id: 01a11658-2655-77e5-a4ff-8bfb98dbdfef
    statement: Pins stay tracked and visible during comment-range targeting,
      including during a gesture; only the Checks highlight is suspended.
  - id: 01a11658-269a-75e9-a036-1e44a8e605e2
    statement: A pin is drawn only when its point lies inside a reported visible
      region, farther from that region's edge than the region's error bound.
  - id: 01a11658-26e2-710b-95ff-8bd8b1ce5948
    statement: Proven translucent coverage never hides a pin.
  - id: 01a11658-2727-76aa-aa7e-0661e92496e9
    statement: Pins never request contour precision work.
  - id: 01a11658-276a-7756-ac0e-1f1e00515e27
    statement: A pinned Widget wholly outside the Preview viewport, or mapped
      outside the visible canvas stage, gets an edge indicator from its current
      rectangle; clicking it opens the thread and never scrolls the Preview.
  - id: 01a11658-27ae-7f31-ac4f-8e5c0d8d7812
    statement: Pins close together on screen merge into a count cluster recomputed
      from current positions, and a cluster never keeps a member whose geometry
      is not current.
