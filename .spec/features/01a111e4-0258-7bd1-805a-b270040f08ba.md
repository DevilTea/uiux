---
id: 01a111e4-0258-7bd1-805a-b270040f08ba
title: Visible Widget geometry
summary: "Widget geometry in the Workbench and the Preview: per-Widget geometry
  streams and the tracked-Widget cap, the mapping from the Preview content box
  to the overlay, pin tracking and visibility, visible-region contours and their
  occlusion classification, and contour precision, refinement and caching."
rules:
  - id: 01a11658-2350-7cfb-8494-8f51efd3f991
    statement: The Workbench maps reported geometry into its overlay from the
      browser's actual rendering of the Preview content box, treating configured
      zoom or scale values as inputs, never as the mapping itself.
  - id: 01a11658-2396-777b-8cad-5c0d947171fb
    statement: Moving, scaling, zooming or transforming the Preview from outside,
      scrolling the Workbench or changing the iframe border never requests new
      geometry or advances a revision; the Workbench reprojects the latest
      accepted geometry while it can still measure the mapping.
  - id: 01a11658-23dd-781d-a0d3-c45cba9446b0
    statement: When the Preview content viewport changes size, the Workbench hides
      every highlight and pin at once and reopens every geometry stream under a
      fresh identity, so geometry measured before the resize never shows.
  - id: 01a11658-2426-777f-8c5f-1b9185054975
    statement: When the Workbench cannot reliably measure how the Preview is
      rendered, it draws no highlight, outline or pin, substitutes no bounding
      box, configured value or last-known mapping, and keeps the structural
      selection.
  - id: 01a11658-246f-778d-b267-a8efa4541d60
    statement: Without a browser API that reports the content-box quad, the
      Workbench accepts its box measurement only after proving every transform
      up the flat tree is a translation with uniform positive scale; any failed
      proof, including an SVG ancestor, means unmeasurable.
  - id: 01a11658-24b8-7910-a7f7-b10b04496a23
    statement: The Preview measures a Widget's box only under the same transform
      proof, and a Widget outside it reports no visible region, so rotated or
      skewed Widgets get no highlight or pin.
  - id: 01a11658-2500-71b1-94b5-88e83fbbc674
    statement: The Workbench tracks outer-mapping changes by events and measures the
      rendered mapping at most once per animation frame per Preview, shared by
      every overlay, with no frame loop while no overlay is shown.
  - id: 01a11658-2545-768a-8d08-2b1547fe7d24
    statement: While pins are the only overlay, the Workbench measures only after a
      change signal or during an animation, so shown pins with nothing changing
      cost no frame callbacks and no messages.
  - id: 01a11658-2588-7f50-b8d5-ffcd4d117e41
    statement: "The Workbench keeps at most one geometry stream per Widget and
      shares its reports with every consumer of that Widget: the highlight, the
      hover outline, pins and a pending composer."
  - id: 01a11658-25cb-7c05-ab57-3d57883de94d
    statement: When demand exceeds the stream cap, streams go first to the open or
      selected thread, the pending composer and the hover candidate, then the
      selection highlight, then pinned Widgets by thread status, newest activity
      and Widget identity.
  - id: 01a11687-2f54-7963-a045-2d546f7dcd85
    statement: Without concurrent geometry streams, a hover candidate takes the only
      stream, and the selection outline stays hidden while a candidate exists.
  - id: 01a11658-2611-7395-92f0-372dd841ed6a
    statement: A thread whose Widget gets no stream draws no pin, never a last-known
      one, and its Comments entry says that it is not shown on the canvas and
      suggests narrowing the pin filter.
  - id: 01a11658-2655-77e5-a4ff-8bfb98dbdfef
    statement: Pins stay tracked and visible during comment-range targeting,
      including during a gesture; only the Checks highlight is suspended.
  - id: 01a11658-269a-75e9-a036-1e44a8e605e2
    statement: A pin is drawn only from its Widget's current accepted report, and
      only when its point lies inside a reported visible region, farther from
      that region's edge than the region's reported error bound.
  - id: 01a11658-26e2-710b-95ff-8bd8b1ce5948
    statement: Proven translucent coverage never hides a pin.
  - id: 01a11658-2727-76aa-aa7e-0661e92496e9
    statement: Pins never request contour precision work.
  - id: 01a11658-276a-7756-ac0e-1f1e00515e27
    statement: A pinned Widget wholly outside the Preview viewport, or mapped
      outside the visible canvas stage, gets an edge indicator from its current
      rectangle; clicking it opens the thread and never scrolls the Preview.
  - id: 01a11658-27ae-7f31-ac4f-8e5c0d8d7812
    statement: Pins close together on screen merge into a count cluster recomputed
      from current positions, and a cluster never keeps a member whose geometry
      is not current.
  - id: 01a11687-3037-74fd-8a8a-0d365206c288
    statement: The Preview reports each disconnected, reliably visible piece of a
      Widget as one closed outer contour, and never computes or reports contours
      of interior holes.
  - id: 01a11687-30c7-7cf6-ade5-5155bded43d6
    statement: The Preview converts quadratic curves and circular or elliptical arcs
      into cubic segments before reporting them, and reports no contour whose
      conversion error it cannot bound.
  - id: 01a11687-3109-7167-9c26-6e51c659d533
    statement: The Preview derives every clipped or occluded outline from the actual
      shape of the clip or occluder, never from a bounding box or a heuristic
      approximation.
  - id: 01a11687-314d-761f-b0a2-a565f419b8ea
    statement: A covering element cuts the Widget's visible region only where the
      Preview proves that the Widget contributes nothing to the covered pixels;
      near-opaque thresholds and style heuristics never suffice.
  - id: 01a11687-3190-7ddd-afc2-6b2fb591f53d
    statement: Coverage that the Preview proves translucent, leaving the Widget
      visibly discernible, keeps the covered area in the visible region and
      marks it as translucently covered; no alpha or contrast threshold decides
      it.
  - id: 01a11687-31d2-72e1-bb13-15fd63ad95b5
    statement: Mixed opaque and translucent coverage is classified area by area from
      each area's own proven status, never by one overlap rule for the whole
      Widget.
  - id: 01a11687-3214-75af-84e6-befd07db432d
    statement: When compositing, masks, filters, an occluder transform outside the
      measurable subset or any other paint over the Widget leave its
      contribution or visible perimeter unproven, the Preview invents no
      classification or contour and reports no precise visible region.
  - id: 01a11687-3256-7814-b6a7-2ab2591d9e72
    statement: The Preview reports when it cannot establish a Widget's visible
      region or contour precision, distinctly from reporting that no part of the
      Widget is visible.
  - id: 01a11687-3297-7504-9206-92cb35788aaf
    statement: Precise clipping supports at least opaque rectangles and rounded
      boxes, clip-path basic shapes and polygons, and geometric SVG clip paths,
      whenever the Preview can derive their actual shape.
  - id: 01a11687-32da-7ea6-83aa-d7451aaf0111
    statement: The Workbench draws a Widget's precise highlight only while every one
      of its contours meets the display tolerance under the current outer
      mapping, and otherwise hides the whole highlight, never only the failing
      regions.
  - id: 01a11687-331e-7b04-98ad-f9f949eebd65
    statement: When an outer change makes a cached contour miss the display
      tolerance, the Workbench requests finer contours for the unchanged
      geometry instead of remeasuring the Widget, and restores the highlight
      only from a result that meets it.
  - id: 01a11687-3366-7961-bb45-c04df9b1fb40
    statement: The Workbench refines only the regions that miss the display
      tolerance, and requests a complete regeneration instead when the cached
      regions cannot be matched by identity in the current geometry.
  - id: 01a11687-33a8-713d-953b-6644aa828037
    statement: The Workbench keeps one complete contour cache per Widget geometry
      revision, and a newly committed cache is never less precise in any region
      than the one it replaces.
  - id: 01a11687-33ee-716f-8463-5979a0af83df
    statement: Merging a partial result keeps, for each region, whichever of the
      cached and returned contours has the smaller error bound, and commits the
      whole merged set at once or not at all.
  - id: 01a11687-3431-7067-9ede-746027543b03
    statement: When a scale change brings the cached contours back within the
      display tolerance while finer contours are pending, the Workbench restores
      the cached highlight at once, without waiting or sending another request.
  - id: 01a11687-3474-7773-b690-0cc1cc06f8ea
    statement: The Workbench retires pending contour work that a restored cache has
      made unnecessary and sends a best-effort cancellation for it.
  - id: 01a11687-34ba-7318-be3c-0ba1348b3063
    statement: After a partial refinement is rejected, misses its precision or fails
      recoverably without a verifiable response, the Workbench retries at once,
      only once, with a new request for the full original region set, reusing
      nothing from the failed attempt.
  - id: 01a11687-34ff-75b5-9f7a-f7b8abf922f4
    statement: When that retry also fails, or the base cache or geometry is no
      longer current, the Workbench requests a complete contour regeneration
      instead of another partial refinement.
  - id: 01a11687-3543-7738-a0bc-690610c77148
    statement: Only a transient failure without a verifiable response, for a
      protocol-valid request in a current context, is recoverable; protocol
      violations, malformed envelopes, unsupported capabilities and permanent
      runtime failures never use the refinement retry.
