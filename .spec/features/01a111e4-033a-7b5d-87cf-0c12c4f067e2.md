title: Device capability tiers
summary: Desktop gets every capability; tablets get review (canvas, comments,
  resolve and reopen, UX Flows and the Prototype player) without structural
  editing; phones get Spec reading plus comment triage and replies, but cannot
  start new canvas comments and cannot Re-anchor a thread or Promote it to a
  Decision, consistently in the canvas thread bubble and the Reviews sheet.
