title: Device capability tiers
summary: What the Workbench offers on desktop, tablet and phone layouts, and how
  the narrower layouts limit authoring and comment actions.
rules:
  - id: 01a116f0-8fd3-7c11-b4d6-85c8653fc8b6
    statement: On a desktop layout the Workbench offers every action the member's
      keys allow.
  - id: 01a116f0-9016-75fb-90ff-ed86412b65a4
    statement: "On a tablet layout the Workbench offers review: the canvas,
      comments, resolving and reopening threads, UX Flows and the Prototype
      player."
  - id: 01a116f0-9059-7ec4-a560-35159d01e56e
    statement: On a tablet layout the Workbench offers no structural editing;
      authoring screens stay read-only and say that editing happens on desktop.
  - id: 01a116f0-909c-7403-a47c-ba9bbefdf0ef
    statement: On a phone layout the Workbench offers reading the Spec and triaging
      and replying to comments, and no structural editing.
  - id: 01a116f0-90e2-7a5a-b9d8-c80984d034ea
    statement: On a phone no new canvas comment can be started.
  - id: 01a116f0-9128-77b3-8800-1a9edf67106c
    statement: On a phone neither the canvas thread bubble nor the Reviews sheet
      offers Re-anchor or Promote to Decision.
  - id: 01a116f0-9170-72ca-872c-a9ca805fc8e1
    statement: On a phone the Reviews inbox opens a thread in a sheet where the
      reviewer reads, replies, resolves and reopens it.
  - id: 01a116f0-91b4-796c-ac7a-d2da4f936935
    statement: On a phone, a fresh load of the bare Overview address opens Reviews,
      while an Overview address that names a tab, or Overview reached by
      navigation, stays on Overview.
  - id: 01a11a5e-1b52-7f6f-8244-65bb038ef6f8
    statement: The version timeline and version diffs are offered on every layout.
  - id: 01a11a5e-1ba5-765e-966f-a681def5f472
    statement: The canvas before/after comparison and Create Checkpoint are offered
      on desktop and tablet layouts, never on a phone.
  - id: 01a11a5e-1bfa-71e9-9611-152b5b665379
    statement: Restore is offered only on a desktop layout.
  - id: 01a11e0d-dada-7f49-a4a0-5b45d70b23e8
    statement: Deleting a Checkpoint is offered only on a desktop layout.

## Notes

- The device classes are named by layout, not by width: the breakpoints in code (desktop at 1280 CSS px and wider, phone below 768, and comment creation also blocked on short coarse-pointer screens, `app/composables/useMediaQuery.ts#L17-L30` at 673d086) are not normative (DESIGN.md, owner ruling Q7). Device tiers only narrow what the member's role allows; the server never sees the device.
- Not decided by ruling B9 and left to code: formal capture and submitting for review are desktop-only, and Handoff export is also offered on tablets (`app/components/readiness/ReadinessTab.vue`, `app/pages/index.vue` at 673d086); PRODUCT.md lists Evidence and Handoff under desktop. Owner ruling 2026-10-07 (batch 7 confirmation): these tier details and the breakpoint values are non-normative, and the code stands.
