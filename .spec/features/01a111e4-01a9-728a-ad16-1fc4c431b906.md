---
id: 01a111e4-01a9-728a-ad16-1fc4c431b906
title: Evidence freshness
summary: "Whether existing formal Evidence still holds for the current
  Workspace: the staleness conditions, and which Evidence a change requires
  regenerating."
rules:
  - id: 01a115cd-c53c-7121-ae74-8243c4890477
    statement: A formal capture that names no View, or whose provenance records no
      View revision, is stale.
  - id: 01a115cd-c571-7baa-8f91-6309737e2b7b
    statement: A formal capture whose View no longer exists is stale.
  - id: 01a115cd-c5a5-757a-b049-b5138fe75920
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its View's current revision differs
      from the revision it recorded.
  - id: 01a115cd-c5d9-7da3-88fa-db434af003e3
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its Locale no longer exists in the
      Workspace.
  - id: 01a115cd-c60d-7581-b3c1-576b81fd3b3e
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its Locale's revision differs from the
      revision it recorded.
  - id: 01a115cd-c641-7bc7-9abd-6f6072e681c8
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its viewport preset is removed.
  - id: 01a115cd-c675-74cd-aaa3-c372a4250cd2
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its viewport preset's width or height
      differs from the size it captured.
  - id: 01a115cd-c6a9-7a09-af68-50b6301a9148
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its theme is removed from the Workspace.
  - id: 01a115cd-c6de-7744-be27-715bec2e7fef
    statement: A formal capture is stale, with the reason that its Adapter changed
      since capture, once its recorded Adapter provenance no longer matches the
      current active Adapter set in selection, order or content.
  - id: 01a1161b-ff68-76af-8237-dbc8ef2c27d1
    statement: A formal capture that records no Adapter provenance has unknown
      freshness, which counts as not fresh.
  - id: 01a115cd-c715-7527-b773-cae2d87c8f33
    statement: Which existing Evidence a change requires regenerating is decided
      from the change's structured change domains, never inferred from prose.
  - id: 01a115cd-c749-7a82-bf67-7d1634e31b35
    statement: Evidence that a change's domains leave demonstrably unaffected stays
      valid and need not be rerun.

## Implementation gaps

- 01a115cd-c6de-7744-be27-715bec2e7fef: Not built as of 85055c1: capture provenance records no Adapter, so an Adapter change never makes Evidence stale; tracked in issue #57 (https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/issues/57).
- 01a1161b-ff68-76af-8237-dbc8ef2c27d1: Not built as of 85055c1: no record carries Adapter provenance and staleness has no unknown outcome, so every capture without it still counts as fresh (`src/domain/evidence/staleness.ts`); tracked in issue #57 (https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/issues/57).
- 01a115cd-c715-7527-b773-cae2d87c8f33: Not built as of 85055c1: staleness ignores change domains: any change to a View's revision makes all its captures stale; tracked in issue #98 (https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/issues/98).
- 01a115cd-c749-7a82-bf67-7d1634e31b35: Not built as of 85055c1: a purely textual Spec change still makes the View's captures stale; tracked in issue #98 (https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/issues/98).

## Notes

- Owner ruling B3 accepted the shipped staleness conditions; the Workspace manifest revision a capture records is not compared. The owner ruling on issue #57 (Discussion #10 c4, 2026-10-07) amends B3: an Adapter provenance mismatch is stale (01a115cd-c6de-7744-be27-715bec2e7fef), and 01a1161b-ff68-76af-8237-dbc8ef2c27d1 is the single owner of unknown freshness, which the readiness, submission and Handoff Rules treat as not fresh. Per-Widget-type Adapter granularity is out of scope (decision 5). The recorded Adapter provenance is owned by 01a1161c-008a-746d-8d53-a8301b23588f. That stale Evidence does not satisfy a submission is owned by 01a11544-558b-7b86-b4c7-76928862c948, and in Handoff by 01a115cd-ca50-70b3-a65c-bd1c9f74aba4.
- The B3 conditions decide whether a record still holds for the current Workspace; 01a115cd-c715-7527-b773-cae2d87c8f33 and 01a115cd-c749-7a82-bf67-7d1634e31b35 decide which Evidence a change requires regenerating (Discussion #1 c159 decisions 1 and 3). Owner ruling 2026-10-07 (batch 5 confirmation): the B3 revision-based conditions remain the freshness criteria, and change-domain regeneration is the accepted, not-yet-built refinement; both coexist.
