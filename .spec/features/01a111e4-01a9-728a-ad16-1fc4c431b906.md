title: Evidence freshness
summary: Whether existing formal evidence still validates a View is decided from
  structured change domains, so only affected captures need to be rerun while
  invalid or stale evidence keeps blocking readiness. A formal evidence record
  is stale when it has no `viewId`, when its View was deleted, when its
  provenance records no View resource or its View revision changed, when its
  locale is missing from the Workspace or its locale revision changed, when its
  viewport preset was removed or its size changed, or when its theme was
  removed.
