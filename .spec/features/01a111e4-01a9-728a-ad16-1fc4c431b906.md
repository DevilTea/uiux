---
id: 01a111e4-01a9-728a-ad16-1fc4c431b906
title: Evidence freshness
summary: "Whether existing formal Evidence still holds for the current
  Workspace: the staleness conditions, and which Evidence a change requires
  regenerating."
rules:
  - id: 01a115cd-c53c-7121-ae74-8243c4890477
    statement: A formal capture that names no View, or whose provenance records no
      View revision or no render-input digest, is stale.
  - id: 01a115cd-c571-7baa-8f91-6309737e2b7b
    statement: A formal capture whose View no longer exists is stale.
  - id: 01a115cd-c5a5-757a-b049-b5138fe75920
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its View's current render-input digest
      (Clause 01a14e30-2d4a-7ce9-ad68-58db56f36f47) differs from the one it
      recorded.
  - id: 01a115cd-c5d9-7da3-88fa-db434af003e3
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its Locale no longer exists in the
      Workspace.
  - id: 01a115cd-c60d-7581-b3c1-576b81fd3b3e
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its Locale's revision differs from the
      revision it recorded.
  - id: 01a115cd-c641-7bc7-9abd-6f6072e681c8
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its viewport preset is removed.
  - id: 01a115cd-c675-74cd-aaa3-c372a4250cd2
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its viewport preset's width or height
      differs from the size it captured.
  - id: 01a115cd-c6a9-7a09-af68-50b6301a9148
    statement: A formal capture is stale once its theme is removed from the Workspace.
  - id: 01a115cd-c6de-7744-be27-715bec2e7fef
    statement: A formal capture is stale, with the reason that its Adapter changed
      since capture, once its recorded Adapter provenance no longer matches the
      current active Adapter set in selection, order, content or configuration.
  - id: 01a1161b-ff68-76af-8237-dbc8ef2c27d1
    statement: A formal capture that records no Adapter provenance, or no Product
      Kit provenance, has unknown freshness, which counts as not fresh.
  - id: 01a164bc-4e09-78be-91fe-e7ecb3c42edb
    statement: "A formal capture has unknown freshness, which counts as not fresh,
      while the current Adapter provenance cannot be computed: the Product Kit
      file is unreadable, the Adapter set is invalid, or an Adapter has neither
      a package version nor a content digest."
  - id: 01a115cd-c715-7527-b773-cae2d87c8f33
    statement: Which existing Evidence a change requires regenerating is decided
      from the change's structured change domains, never inferred from prose.
  - id: 01a115cd-c749-7a82-bf67-7d1634e31b35
    statement: Evidence that a change's domains leave demonstrably unaffected stays
      valid and need not be rerun.
  - id: 01a11bb1-be4a-7f9b-9fd6-a6e71241113c
    statement: A formal capture is stale, with the reason that the Product Kit
      changed since capture, once the data or output digest of its recorded
      Product Kit provenance differs from the current one.

Ruling 8 of the Discussion #139 owner rulings of 2026-10-10 settles it like the #57 ruling: such a capture is unknown, hence not fresh, and needs a recapture. 01a1161b-ff68-76af-8237-dbc8ef2c27d1 stays the single owner of unknown freshness and now covers both provenances.
- Discussion #241 R2 (accepted 2026-10-18) resolves the conflict between 01a115cd-c5a5-757a-b049-b5138fe75920 and 01a115cd-c749-7a82-bf67-7d1634e31b35: a capture follows what was rendered, the View's IR and Variants (01a14e30-2d4a-7ce9-ad68-58db56f36f47), so a Spec-only edit, or a rename of the View's `name` or `feature`, leaves its Evidence fresh, and an IR or Variant change makes it stale. The other conditions (Locale, viewport, theme, Adapter, Product Kit) are unchanged. This answers the question issue #56 leaves to Part 10; the rename operation itself stays the scoped task of issue #56. The ready-for-review check follows the same digest (01a11544-558b-7b86-b4c7-76928862c948).
