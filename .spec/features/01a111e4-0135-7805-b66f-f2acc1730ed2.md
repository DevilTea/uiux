title: Reference integrity and destructive changes
summary: Destructive or identity-changing operations (deleting a View, UX Flow
  or Asset; renaming a Variant or a render-dimension key) first report their
  known reference impact and proceed only after explicit acknowledgement; they
  stay target-local, never cascade or auto-rebind, and leave dangling references
  as repairable diagnostics.
