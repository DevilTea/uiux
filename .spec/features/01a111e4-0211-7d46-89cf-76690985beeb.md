title: Formal UX Flow Evidence
summary: "Formal Evidence for a UX Flow: the replayable traversal trace and its
  captures, the Evidence-owned replay recipe and its immutability, and how
  Review and Handoff use Flow Evidence."
rules:
  - id: 01a11544-6628-73b9-b10a-9643d264bea1
    statement: Formal UX Flow Evidence is a deterministic, replayable trace with its
      required Playwright captures, recording the entry context and, per
      traversed step, its identity, View and Variant, triggering Widget Event,
      resulting step, validation result and Evidence references.
  - id: 01a11544-6655-7786-8185-f3e8c8d2c706
    statement: UIUX executes and verifies the playback with Playwright; a manual
      screenshot sequence or prose walkthrough is never formal Flow Evidence.
  - id: 01a11544-6681-7387-bdc3-9b825261f887
    statement: A trace identifies each transition by its source step, trigger and
      resulting step, never by a transition ID.
  - id: 01a11544-66ad-73e5-8fcd-9e24bfcb3313
    statement: The Playwright actions and assertions that drive a replay belong to
      the Evidence record, never to the Flow or to Adapter Event metadata.
  - id: 01a11544-66d9-7fcd-8400-93516ddf2857
    statement: A replay must show that its recorded actions produce the expected
      Widget Event and transition, so the Evidence proves the semantic
      transition rather than browser operations.
  - id: 01a11544-6707-70a7-b544-590b4f1e8a91
    statement: "A replay recipe is immutable with its Evidence: changing it creates
      new Evidence, and a copied earlier recipe is only an authoring starting
      point."
  - id: 01a11544-6734-7a24-a957-e23ddeb3e396
    statement: A Handoff manifest references Flow traces and captures as derived
      artifacts, while the canonical Flow is an authored Handoff resource.
  - id: 01a11544-6760-7ec2-8430-0f7cc5807259
    statement: A Review submission may name UX Flow as a change domain and reference
      specific Flows and their traces.
