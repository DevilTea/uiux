---
id: 01a111e4-014a-7438-897b-ba59bc67edca
title: View Spec Decisions
summary: "Decisions recorded in a View's Spec: their identity, lifecycle and
  history, the derived lists of pending Decisions, and explicit promotion of a
  Review thread into a Decision."
rules:
  - id: 01a11544-5e41-79bd-8fb8-5a632bacdfaf
    statement: A View's Decisions all live in one list in that View's Spec, whatever
      their status.
  - id: 01a115cd-3119-7728-ad0c-87b77b67b34f
    statement: Pending, deferred and decided Decision lists are derived from that
      one list and never stored separately.
  - id: 01a11544-5e6e-7af9-8557-a9702bc8ca23
    statement: A Decision keeps its identity through every status change; deferring,
      deciding or reopening never moves or re-creates it.
  - id: 01a11544-5e99-789d-bfed-dda22a98776f
    statement: A Decision's status changes only through explicit lifecycle actions,
      never implicitly.
  - id: 01a11544-5ec3-73ae-b122-ed8387eff75b
    statement: Reopening a decided Decision clears its current outcome, and the
      earlier outcome survives only in its history.
  - id: 01a11544-5eee-7ebe-90f1-14e954dbd7f9
    statement: Every status or outcome change appends a history entry recording its
      time, its actor or source and the state before and after, so a Decision's
      lifecycle never depends on Git.
  - id: 01a11544-5f18-77ac-b89e-56ced4056f19
    statement: The Workbench derives a cross-View list of pending Decisions from the
      View files and navigates from each item to its View, and to its Variant or
      Widget when known.
  - id: 01a11544-5f40-707b-9afc-695cd41d04bb
    statement: No reply, resolution or reopen of a Review thread changes or settles
      a Decision.
  - id: 01a11544-5f68-7e07-a7e0-fa99257570f6
    statement: A Review thread becomes a Decision only through an explicit promotion
      by a human or Agent; comments are never turned into Decisions
      automatically.
  - id: 01a11544-5f90-7b18-a1b6-48c2bd167100
    statement: A thread is promoted only into the View it is anchored to.
  - id: 01a11544-5fb8-7547-b41a-ded1e2a23a8a
    statement: A Workspace thread cannot be promoted; it must first be re-anchored
      to a View's RootShell.
  - id: 01a11544-5fe0-76ac-a2ad-ae86e40c72b5
    statement: A promoted Decision starts pending unless the promotion records an
      outcome, which makes it decided.
  - id: 01a11544-6009-713a-8335-12c872294026
    statement: A promoted Decision records its source thread's identity, and the
      Workbench navigates from the Decision back to that thread.
  - id: 01a11544-6032-772b-b429-36a30800d70d
    statement: Promoting a thread that already has a Decision in its View returns
      that Decision instead of creating a duplicate.
  - id: 01a11544-605b-7409-a010-fe345fb0284b
    statement: That idempotent return comes before revision checks, so retrying a
      committed promotion with its original expected revisions succeeds.
  - id: 01a11544-6084-7f2c-848d-cc4319e66134
    statement: Creating another, independent Decision from the same thread requires
      a distinct explicit action.
  - id: 01a11544-60bb-7018-8c81-82c17e5b80ed
    statement: Promotion checks the thread's and the View's revisions and adds the
      Decision to the View in one atomic commit, leaving the thread unchanged.
  - id: 01a11544-60ee-7323-bc78-c20a8c2e6a2b
    statement: "Promote and resolve stay two actions with their own revision checks:
      there is no compound promote-and-resolve action and no promoted resolution
      kind."
  - id: 01a11544-611c-73e0-990b-c094b48b3eb6
    statement: When a promotion conflicts, the Workbench names which resource moved,
      the thread or the View, re-reads and asks the user to confirm again.
---
