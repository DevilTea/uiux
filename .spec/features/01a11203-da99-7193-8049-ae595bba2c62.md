---
id: 01a11203-da99-7193-8049-ae595bba2c62
title: Review thread retraction
summary: "Hard deletion of a brand-new Review thread before anyone engages with
  it: who may retract, when a thread is eligible, what remains afterwards, and
  the Workbench affordance."
rules:
  - id: 01a11544-598c-7217-8b56-7cb8ff0236da
    statement: Only the author of a thread's single message may retract the thread,
      whether a human or an Agent.
  - id: 01a11544-59b5-7bb1-a605-4985ac75c208
    statement: A thread whose message was written before actors were stamped cannot
      be retracted, because no stamp can prove its authorship.
  - id: 01a11544-59df-7822-9aba-ff39da0d826b
    statement: A thread can be retracted only while it is open, holds at most one
      message, has no history events or submissions, and no Decision was
      promoted from it.
  - id: 01a11544-5a05-7750-9268-fd666980dd2f
    statement: A thread with no messages, which has no author, may be retracted by
      any Reviewer under the same conditions.
  - id: 01a11544-5a2e-716b-9a06-c910418f24ac
    statement: The author's own edits and any move of the pin are not engagement and
      never block retraction.
  - id: 01a11544-5a55-7249-948f-4e136df52a63
    statement: "Retraction removes the thread file atomically and leaves no trace in
      canonical state: no tombstone, history entry or record of who retracted
      it."
  - id: 01a11544-5a7f-7e63-9379-ddd56dc82792
    statement: Eligibility is checked on exactly the thread revision being deleted,
      inside the delete's exclusive lock, so a racing reply or promotion either
      lands first and makes retraction refuse, or finds the thread gone.
  - id: 01a11544-5aa5-76bd-99a9-b8b9b0599a03
    statement: After retraction, reads and writes of the thread report it as not found.
  - id: 01a11544-5acc-7e09-9b85-aca405d1d364
    statement: Copies of a retracted thread inside already-exported Handoff bundles
      remain; retraction never scans or rewrites artifacts.
  - id: 01a11544-5af4-71eb-b8e4-917743937508
    statement: The Workbench offers to delete a comment only to its author while the
      loaded thread is eligible, and offers to delete an empty thread to any
      Reviewer.
  - id: 01a11544-5b1b-7dcb-bcea-e36f4ffa3b2a
    statement: The Workbench confirms a deletion in an inline row with focus on
      Cancel, never in a browser dialog.
  - id: 01a11544-5b42-7ca4-a501-a1c4977a6ccd
    statement: A link to a retracted thread opens the inbox's missing-thread state,
      or on the canvas the View with a notice that the comment no longer exists.
