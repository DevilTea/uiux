title: Review thread retraction
summary: The author of a brand-new Review thread, human or Agent, may
  hard-delete it without trace while it is `open`, holds only the author's one
  message and has no history, submissions or promoted Decision; an empty,
  authorless thread may be retracted by any Reviewer under the same conditions;
  once anyone engages the thread can only be dismissed, and copies inside
  already-exported Handoff bundles remain.
