title: Preview failure diagnostics
summary: "Non-recoverable Preview failures: the refinement failure latch and
  what clears it, the inline Preview diagnostic with its copy and technical
  details, and the manual Retry Preview action."
rules:
  - id: 01a11687-35cb-7bf2-85a8-1d304182cdc3
    statement: A non-recoverable failure of contour refinement ends that refinement
      and never starts a complete contour regeneration solely because of that
      failure.
  - id: 01a11687-360d-7eea-bbbe-8e6275cc73aa
    statement: After a non-recoverable refinement failure, while the cache misses
      the display tolerance, the Workbench keeps the highlight hidden and the
      Widget selected, and shows that precise highlighting is unavailable.
  - id: 01a11687-3650-7490-a7ec-80b2570582f6
    statement: After a non-recoverable refinement failure, no new refinement starts
      within the same geometry revision until the geometry or context changes, a
      recovered runtime generation is confirmed or an explicit retry is taken.
  - id: 01a11687-3693-723e-8f71-82c431d79220
    statement: Outer scale changes alone never clear the refinement failure latch or
      start a refinement.
  - id: 01a11687-36d8-771c-ab58-2d564fd20f5b
    statement: While refinement is latched, cached contours that meet the display
      tolerance at the current scale are still drawn, and precise highlighting
      shows as unavailable again once they no longer do.
  - id: 01a11687-371a-72fd-aac3-8e32a7ab9233
    statement: Runtime or capability recovery clears the latch only when a new
      runtime generation declares its capabilities again; elapsed time,
      reconnects, health checks, scale changes and further traffic of the same
      generation never do.
  - id: 01a11687-375e-7667-b8f1-59389fdd9780
    statement: While the Preview session continues but geometry or refinement is
      unavailable after a non-recoverable failure, the Workbench shows a
      persistent, non-modal inline diagnostic in the Preview or selection
      context until recovery, context replacement or session end.
  - id: 01a11687-37a0-77bb-a857-5f7804b7c306
    statement: The diagnostic takes its title from the failure category and its body
      from the recognized reason, describing the impact and the available
      recovery, both from canonical Workbench translations.
  - id: 01a11687-37e4-76ef-8468-35e2e9d67a4d
    statement: For a reason it does not recognize, the diagnostic uses its
      category's generic title and body and shows the raw reason only in its
      expandable technical details.
  - id: 01a11687-3828-7160-b15b-13827988070e
    statement: The diagnostic's primary message states the failure category and its
      current impact; the reason and the applicable correlation identities
      appear only in expandable technical details, and raw stack traces or
      exception dumps never appear.
  - id: 01a11687-386c-764f-98fd-cf19dc3d0013
    statement: Every failure eligible for manual recovery in the same Preview
      session offers one shared Retry Preview action, never a reason-specific
      label, and activating it is an explicit retry.
  - id: 01a11687-38b0-706c-9cf7-613152b11492
    statement: While the generation that Retry Preview authorized is still in its
      handshake, the action is disabled.
  - id: 01a11687-38f9-7e76-ac7a-4924dfcceea1
    statement: Superseded contour work that a cancellation fails to stop never
      produces a Preview or Checks diagnostic.

## Notes

- Which failures the Workbench derives itself, as of 3931ea3 (issue #111, orchestrator decision of 2026-10-23 on its scope): a capability failure keeps its `capability.*` reason, a conflicting declaration is `protocol.capability_conflict`, an undecodable message before the handshake opens is `protocol.invalid_message`, a declaration naming a retired generation is `protocol.identity_reuse` only while no generation is open, and a generation refused while recovery is exhausted keeps the diagnostic already shown. Nothing derives `transport.channel_unavailable` yet. The translated copy lives in the Workbench catalogs under `previewFailure`.
- The wire side is owned by the Preview protocol Contract: categories by 01a11687-4088-7e56-81f9-41d1536ed88c, reason codes by 01a11687-40cf-7a08-a3f6-2549ebd4801a and 01a11687-4114-728a-9d58-d69eaf786e5d, and the diagnostic's members by 01a11687-415c-79d5-9978-62683f35e9e2. The retry authorization that Retry Preview spends is owned by the session Feature (01a1161c-3cf8-7859-9de8-2b99d22ce8aa, 01a1161c-3e28-7dcf-a06e-a2c12c83919c); which refinement failures are recoverable by 01a11687-3543-7738-a0bc-690610c77148.
- Reading of Discussion #1 c118-c120: the "explicit new retry action to be specified later" is the Retry Preview action of #2 c15 and c32, whose fresh generation and renewed declaration satisfy 01a11687-371a-72fd-aac3-8e32a7ab9233.
- Not imported: the translated copy itself (Discussion #2 c32: locale strings stay canonical Workbench translations) and developer telemetry or logging of ineffective cancellation (#2 c33 d3, optional).
- Discussion #131 gives runtime-detected failures a carrier, `failure.report` (01a11a59-dd69-78ed-9181-d8beb5f4cb7b, 01a11a59-def9-797a-8d08-5e4b02ecd900, 01a11a70-4261-7155-8aed-28bed7a89064); the Workbench derives transport, capability and handshake failures itself. A work-scoped report is a non-recoverable refinement failure under 01a11687-35cb-7bf2-85a8-1d304182cdc3 and 01a11687-3650-7490-a7ec-80b2570582f6, and a generation-scoped one shows the inline diagnostic of 01a11687-375e-7667-b8f1-59389fdd9780. View and Adapter load errors are content errors shown by the Preview and Checks, never Preview failures (#131 R28). `failure.report` is tracked in issue #312 (https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/issues/312).
- A region status is not a failure (#131 "Why Group B and Group C do not share a carrier"): an `unavailable` status (01a11a59-dbda-79e5-9337-307bb9c1798b) is shown as precise highlighting unavailable, without the inline diagnostic or Retry Preview.
