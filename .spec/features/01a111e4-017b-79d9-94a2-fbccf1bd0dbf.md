summary: "The content-addressed store of UIUX-derived artifacts: who produces
  and reads artifacts, and when they may be removed."
rules:
  - id: 01a115cd-c16d-7dc8-a770-b6455ab80409
    statement: Derived artifacts are created only by producer actions such as
      capture, checks, Evidence, Handoff export and Checkpoint creation; no Tool
      or route offers a generic artifact write or overwrite.
  - id: 01a115cd-c1a0-72bb-a4f9-1f5063b5d33e
    statement: Artifacts are read by their content identity, never through a path or
      a mutable latest name.
  - id: 01a115cd-c1d4-73fe-b093-c9d1c2226916
    statement: Normal capture, check and mutation actions never delete artifacts.
  - id: 01a115cd-c208-7a92-9582-69966f36e7fe
    statement: Artifact cleanup is an explicit mark-and-sweep that runs only through
      the command of Clause 01a14e30-30de-70c3-888f-63e8982353d7 and removes
      only blobs that no root of Rule 01a14e30-31c0-7289-a944-74af9556eabf
      reaches.
  - id: 01a14e30-31c0-7289-a944-74af9556eabf
    statement: Artifact cleanup's roots are the Evidence that a Review submission
      references, the blobs that a Checkpoint or this host's history of the
      Workspace (Clause 01a11a5e-1e65-7deb-a89e-6ad021248fe0) references and,
      for each View and render context, the latest Evidence record; Handoff
      blobs are not roots.
  - id: 01a115cd-c23e-7143-bd1e-7ffd9fcaa6f2
    statement: Artifact cleanup never rewrites a canonical record.

## Notes

- The store path, identity, immutability and deduplication, and Evidence records as artifacts, are owned by 01a1144e-5508-7eda-a9a0-23fb0d3ebabd, 01a1144e-551f-7352-8973-3d3debe31ee7, 01a1144e-5537-7dbd-8b9e-db2e0634aace and 01a115cd-ce92-7986-b9a6-0e5fa9e092f5; reading an artifact over MCP by 01a11485-f70a-7d29-9b48-e7df3e634a54. Copies inside exported bundles survive Review retraction (01a11544-5acc-7e09-9b85-aca405d1d364).
- Discussion #241 R3 (accepted 2026-10-18) defines the roots (01a14e30-31c0-7289-a944-74af9556eabf) because Evidence records are themselves store blobs and no canonical record names a View's current Evidence: a sweep from canonical records alone would delete fresh Evidence, so the latest Evidence record of each View and render context is a root. Handoff blobs are not roots; a bundle copied out of the store keeps its own copies (01a11544-5acc-7e09-9b85-aca405d1d364). The command is 01a14e30-30de-70c3-888f-63e8982353d7.
- How the roots are read ([#241 owner decisions for #251 and #252](https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/discussions/241#discussioncomment-19019221), as of 884a5d1). An Evidence record carries no capture time. So "the latest Evidence record" of a View and render context follows the version timeline of the View revision each record names. That revision's place is the latest time a Checkpoint, a host version or one of its write events, the open autosave, or a Review submission records it. The View's current revision is newer than all of them. A revision that is re-seen only during a recording gap, or only on another host, is placed at its older time. Blob modification times are not used. Among the records of one Evidence kind and execution context, the ones at the newest place are kept. When the order cannot be decided, every record of the group is kept. That happens when a record names no single revision of its View, when a record's revision is not on the timeline (unless the newest is the current revision), when two revisions share the newest place, or when the context names no View. A kept blob keeps every `sha256:<64 hex>` identity found anywhere in its bytes, and in its parsed value when it is JSON, recursively through every kept blob, whether or not it is a valid Evidence record. A Review thread, a Checkpoint, a host version, the open autosave journal or a stored blob that the cleanup cannot read refuses the whole run before anything is deleted, because it could name any blob. So does a host history directory that cannot be opened or is incomplete. Only regular files at `artifacts/sha256/<2>/<64>` under their own shard are candidates. A symbolic link is never one. The sweep deletes each blob only after every doomed blob that references it. [code@884a5d1 `src/persistence/artifact-gc.ts#L145-L178`](https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/blob/884a5d1c56b25a2fe67a3312a35ca5e1e54b7980/src/persistence/artifact-gc.ts#L145-L178), [code@884a5d1 `src/persistence/artifact-gc.ts#L260-L336`](https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/blob/884a5d1c56b25a2fe67a3312a35ca5e1e54b7980/src/persistence/artifact-gc.ts#L260-L336).
- The cleanup sees only this host's history. A cleanup on another clone, after a Checkpoint is deleted, can remove that Checkpoint's blobs, and the deletion reaches this host through a commit. Since issue #253 (https://github.com/DevilTea/uiux/issues/253) a host version keeps every blob it names in host history (Clause 01a11a5e-1e65-7deb-a89e-6ad021248fe0), so this host's versions stay comparable and restorable; as of e2bf40e.
