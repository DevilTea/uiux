title: Content-addressed artifact store
summary: "The content-addressed store of UIUX-derived artifacts: who produces
  and reads artifacts, and when they may be removed."
rules:
  - id: 01a115cd-c16d-7dc8-a770-b6455ab80409
    statement: Derived artifacts are created only by producer actions such as
      capture, checks, Evidence, Handoff export and checkpoint creation; no Tool
      or route offers a generic artifact write or overwrite.
  - id: 01a115cd-c1a0-72bb-a4f9-1f5063b5d33e
    statement: Artifacts are read by their content identity, never through a path or
      a mutable latest name.
  - id: 01a115cd-c1d4-73fe-b093-c9d1c2226916
    statement: Normal capture, check and mutation actions never delete artifacts.
  - id: 01a115cd-c208-7a92-9582-69966f36e7fe
    statement: Artifact cleanup is an explicit mark-and-sweep maintenance action
      that removes only blobs unreachable from surviving Evidence, Review,
      checkpoint and still-relevant Handoff references.
  - id: 01a115cd-c23e-7143-bd1e-7ffd9fcaa6f2
    statement: Artifact cleanup never rewrites a canonical record.
